# Week 11 – Lecture 2: Inventory Management (Contd.)

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Selective Inventory Control:** Categorizing thousands of warehouse stock-keeping units (SKUs) into prioritized tiers to allocate managerial focus, counting frequency, and capital expenditure efficiently.
- **ABC Analysis:** A selective control technique based on Vilfredo Pareto’s 80/20 rule, classifying inventory items according to their **Annual Dollar Usage** ($\text{Unit Cost} \times \text{Annual Consumption}$).
- **Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI):** A collaborative replenishment model where the upstream supplier monitors the buyer's warehouse inventory levels and takes full responsibility for generating replenishment purchase orders.

## 2. Important Points
- **The ABC Inventory Classification Breakdown:**
  - **Class A Items (Top $10\text{–}20\%$ of SKUs):**
    - Account for **$70\text{–}80\%$ of total annual inventory spending**.
    - *Management:* Tight daily control, continuous monitoring, minimal safety stock, accurate demand forecasting, senior manager approval for orders.
  - **Class B Items (Middle $30\%$ of SKUs):**
    - Account for **$15\text{–}20\%$ of annual inventory spending**.
    - *Management:* Moderate periodic review, automated re-ordering rules.
  - **Class C Items (Bottom $50\text{–}60\%$ of SKUs):**
    - Account for only **$5\text{–}10\%$ of annual inventory spending** (bulk hardware, washers).
    - *Management:* Simple visual control (Two-Bin system), large bulk safety buffers, low managerial intervention.
- **Other Selective Control Classifications:**
  - *VED Analysis (Vital, Essential, Desirable):* Based on criticality of spare parts for equipment uptime.
  - *FSN Analysis (Fast-moving, Slow-moving, Non-moving):* Based on inventory consumption velocity.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Item Class | Proportion of Total SKUs | Share of Annual Spend | Control Rigor | Counting Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Class A** | $10\text{–}20\%$ | $70\text{–}80\%$ | Extremely Strict | Daily or Weekly cycle counts |
| **Class B** | $30\%$ | $15\text{–}20\%$ | Moderate | Monthly review |
| **Class C** | $50\text{–}60\%$ | $5\text{–}10\%$ | Low (Two-Bin visual) | Annual physical count |

## 4. Must Remember
- ABC analysis is based on **Annual Dollar Usage**, NOT unit purchase price alone: a cheap 50-cent bolt used in millions of engines can easily become a Class A item.
- VMI eliminates the bullwhip effect by giving the manufacturer direct visibility into real-time retail consumption.
