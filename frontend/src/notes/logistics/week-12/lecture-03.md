# Week 12 – Lecture 3: Assets Management in Supply Chain

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Supply Chain Asset Management:** The strategic and operational lifecycle management of all physical, technological, and intellectual assets employed across a supply chain network to maximize operational uptime, productivity, and financial return on assets.
- **Asset Categories:**
  - *Tangible Physical Assets:* Factories, warehouses, material handling equipment (MHE: forklifts, cranes, automated conveyors), transport fleets (trucks, ships, containers), and physical inventory.
  - *Intangible Assets:* Enterprise software (ERP, WMS, TMS), intellectual property, supply chain analytics algorithms, brand reputation, customer data, and employee operational expertise.
- **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for Assets:** Encompassing initial capital procurement, commissioning, preventive maintenance, energy/fuel consumption, and terminal salvage/decommissioning value.

## 2. Important Points
- **Core Asset Performance Metrics:**
  - **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE):** The global gold-standard benchmark measuring manufacturing and warehouse equipment productivity:
    $$\text{OEE} = \text{Availability} \times \text{Performance} \times \text{Quality}$$
    Where:
    - *Availability:* $\frac{\text{Actual Operating Time}}{\text{Planned Production Time}}$
    - *Performance:* $\frac{\text{Total Output Produced}}{\text{Theoretical Target Output}}$
    - *Quality:* $\frac{\text{Good Defect-Free Units}}{\text{Total Units Produced}}$
    - *World-Class OEE Benchmark:* $\ge 85\%$ ($A \ge 90\%, P \ge 95\%, Q \ge 99.9\%$).
  - **Return on Assets (ROA):** Measures how efficiently capital assets generate net operating income:
    $$\text{ROA} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Assets}}$$
  - **Cash-to-Cash Cycle Time (C2C):** Number of days capital remains tied up between paying suppliers for raw materials and collecting cash from customers:
    $$\text{C2C} = \text{Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO)} + \text{Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)} - \text{Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)}$$

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Asset Category | Asset Examples | Key Management Strategy | Primary Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed Tangible Assets** | Warehouses, automated sorting hubs, trucks | Predictive maintenance, high capacity utilization | Rapid technological obsolescence, idle capital |
| **Working Capital Assets**| Physical raw materials and finished goods inventory | Lean JIT, EOQ optimization, ABC control | Holding costs, damage, product expiration |
| **Intangible Assets** | WMS/TMS software, supply chain routing algorithms | Continuous digital upgrades, cybersecurity | Cyberattacks, software integration failures |
