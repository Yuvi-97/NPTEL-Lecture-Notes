# Week 1 – Lecture 2: Stakeholders in Logistics and Modern Concepts

## 1. Key Concepts

- **Logistics Management:** Planning, implementing, and controlling the efficient flow and storage of goods, services, information, and resources across the supply chain.
- **Logistics Network:** Includes suppliers, manufacturers, warehouses, retailers, transportation providers, and customers.
- **Logistics Design:** Strategic planning of suppliers, manufacturing plants, warehouse locations and capacities, transportation modes, and distribution networks.

## 2. Important Points

### Major Logistics Activities
- **Procurement:** Negotiating order quantity, frequency, volume, and price with suppliers.
- **Inventory Management:** Maintain sufficient stock to meet expected and unexpected demand; avoid excess inventory and stockouts.
- **Warehousing:** Balance customer proximity and faster delivery against warehouse, manpower, and inventory costs. Location and capacity are long-term decisions.
- **Order Processing:** Recording and processing customer orders.
- **Order Tracking:** Providing updates on order acceptance, processing, dispatch, and delivery.
- **Customer Service / Reverse Logistics:** Handle returns, repairs, spare parts, recycling, reuse, and remanufacturing.
- **Technology:** RFID, barcodes, GPS, and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) support tracking, inventory management, and delivery.

### Modern Logistics Concepts
- **Lean Logistics:** Eliminate waste, such as unnecessary transportation, excess inventory, overproduction, waiting, and extra movement.
- **Just-in-Time (JIT):** Receive materials when required, minimizing inventory. Requires reliable nearby suppliers and accurate demand forecasting.
- **Agile Logistics:** Emphasizes flexibility and responsiveness through rapid information sharing.
- **Green Logistics:** Reduce carbon footprint through eco-friendly transportation, efficient vehicle utilization, and renewable energy.
- **Collaborative Logistics:** Stakeholders cooperate and share information across the supply chain.
- **Omnichannel Logistics:** Coordinate orders and fulfilment across multiple sales channels.
- **Reverse Logistics:** Manage returned products for repair, reuse, recycling, or remanufacturing.
- **Supply Chain Visibility:** Track goods and share timely information across the supply chain.

## 4. Must Remember

- **Transportation:** Contributes approximately 50–55% of costs, according to the lecture.
- **Safety stock:** Helps prevent production interruptions caused by supply delays.
- **Warehouse trade-off:** More warehouses can improve delivery speed but increase operating costs and total inventory.
- **Effective logistics design:** Minimize costs, maximize customer satisfaction, improve sustainability, and mitigate risks.
- **Market expansion:** Requires a distribution network capable of reaching the target market.
