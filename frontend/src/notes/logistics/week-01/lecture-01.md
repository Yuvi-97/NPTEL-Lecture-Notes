# Week 1 – Lecture 1: Introduction to Logistics

## 1. Key Concepts

- **Logistics:** Planning, implementing, and controlling the efficient movement and storage of goods, information, and resources.
- **Supply Chain Management (SCM):** A systematic approach that manages the entire supply chain as one integrated system.
- **Relationship:** Logistics is a subset of supply chain management, focusing on the movement and storage of goods and related information.
- **Importance:** Logistics accounts for a major share of supply chain costs; the lecture cites approximately 60–70%.

## 2. Important Points

### Seven Rights of Logistics
Deliver:
1. Right product
2. Right quantity
3. Right condition
4. Right place
5. Right time
6. Right customer
7. Right information

**Goal:** Meet customer expectations while controlling costs and maintaining efficiency.

### Five Key Elements of Logistics
1. **Transportation:** Moving goods between locations using suitable transport modes.
2. **Warehousing:** Storing products to meet demand fluctuations.
3. **Inventory Control:** Maintaining appropriate stock levels; avoiding excess inventory and stockouts.
4. **Order Fulfilment:** Processing customer orders, preparing goods, packaging, and arranging shipment.
5. **Distribution:** Delivering products through suitable channels to customers.

### Evolution of Logistics
- **Local:** Exchange within local communities.
- **Regional/National:** Distribution across wider geographical areas.
- **Global:** International sourcing, transportation, and distribution.
- **Modern:** E-commerce, faster deliveries, product returns, and emerging technologies such as AI, machine learning, and IoT.

## 3. Differences / Examples

| Logistics | Supply Chain Management |
|---|---|
| Focuses on movement and storage of goods and related information. | Manages the entire supply chain as an integrated system. |

**Example:** Wheat moves from farmers to flour mills and then to bread manufacturers. Logistics supports transportation and storage between these stakeholders.

## 4. Must Remember

- **Inventory objective:** Avoid both excess stock and stockouts.
- **Logistics objective:** Deliver the correct product to the right place and customer at the appropriate time while minimizing expenses and maximizing effectiveness.
- **Customer expectations:** Speed, quality, availability, variety, and reliable delivery.
- **Modern challenge:** Balance delivery responsiveness with operational efficiency and cost.
- Logistics management improves customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and competitiveness.
