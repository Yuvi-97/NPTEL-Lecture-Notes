# Week 4 – Lecture 2: Trends in Logistics (Contd.)

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Supply Chain Agility:** The operational capability of a logistics network to rapidly detect, adapt, and cost-effectively respond to unanticipated supply shocks, demand swings, and global disruptions.
- **Logistics 4.0 / Digitalization:** The comprehensive deployment of Industry 4.0 technologies—Internet of Things (IoT), Big Data predictive analytics, Cloud SaaS, and Artificial Intelligence—across logistics operations.
- **Warehouse Automation & Robotics:** Replacing manual handling with Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs), Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs), and Automated Storage and Retrieval Systems (AS/RS).

## 2. Important Points
- **Primary Driving Forces Reshaping Logistics:**
  1. *Agility & Resilience:* Moving from rigid long-term forecasts to dynamic, flexible re-routing and multi-sourcing to withstand geopolitical and climate shocks.
  2. *Real-Time End-to-End Visibility:* IoT sensor tags (RFID, BLE, GPS) providing live temperature, humidity, and location telemetry for pharmaceuticals and perishables.
  3. *Predictive AI & Big Data:* Machine learning algorithms forecasting localized neighborhood demand spikes and dynamically optimizing dynamic freight consolidation.
  4. *Sustainability Imperatives:* Mandates to decarbonize logistics via electric commercial vehicles (EVs), hydrogen fuel cell trucks, and carbon offset tracking.
- **Persistent Macro Challenges:**
  - Critical shortages of certified commercial truck drivers and skilled warehouse technicians.
  - Cybersecurity risks: ransomware and data breaches compromising cloud-connected supply chain platforms.
  - Severe urban traffic congestion inflating last-mile delivery costs.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Operational Aspect | Legacy Logistics Approach | Logistics 4.0 Approach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tracking Method** | Periodic barcode milestone scans at hubs | Continuous real-time IoT sensor telemetry |
| **Warehouse Picking** | Manual paper pick lists and manual pallet jacks | Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) and pick-to-light automation |
| **Planning Horizon** | Static weekly/monthly routing schedules | Dynamic real-time AI dispatch adjusting to traffic and weather |

## 4. Must Remember
- Real-time visibility is not just about tracking parcels; it allows logistics operators to proactively resolve delivery exceptions before customer complaints occur.
- High levels of warehouse automation require substantial upfront capital investment but yield dramatic improvements in picking accuracy and throughput speed.
