# Week 6 – Lecture 1: Supply Chain Uncertainty and Processes

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Implied Demand Uncertainty (Chopra & Meindl):** The resulting uncertainty that a supply chain must plan for, dictated by the specific customer needs the supply chain targets (e.g., short lead times, wide product variety, or high service level guarantee) rather than pure market demand variance alone.
- **Supply Uncertainty:** The risk of unexpected disruptions in raw material availability, supplier manufacturing yield, component defects, and transit delays.
- **Supply Chain Macro Processes:** Three overarching enterprise software and business processes that integrate all cross-company workflows: **CRM**, **ISCM**, and **SRM**.

## 2. Important Points
- **The Three Supply Chain Macro Processes:**
  1. **Customer Relationship Management (CRM):** Processes at the customer-firm boundary designed to generate and fulfill customer demand (marketing, pricing, sales force automation, commercial order capture, and customer service call centers).
  2. **Internal Supply Chain Management (ISCM):** In-house planning and conversion processes managing internal operations (strategic network design, demand forecasting, aggregate production planning, factory scheduling, and inventory deployment).
  3. **Supplier Relationship Management (SRM):** Upstream processes managing external vendors (supplier identification, contract negotiation, purchase order placement, design co-development, and vendor collaboration).
- **Implied Uncertainty Drivers:** Implied demand uncertainty escalates when lead time requirements shorten, product variety increases, innovative new products are introduced, or order sizes fluctuate wildly.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Macro Process | Primary Interface | Core Functional Modules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SRM (Supplier Relationship Management)** | Firm $\longleftrightarrow$ Tier-1 Vendors | Strategic sourcing, supplier scorecards, purchase orders |
| **ISCM (Internal Supply Chain Management)** | Intra-firm Operations | S&OP, Master Production Scheduling (MPS), inventory |
| **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** | Firm $\longleftrightarrow$ End Market / Clients | Pricing, sales order entry, customer service management |

## 4. Must Remember
- Reference text: **Sunil Chopra & Peter Meindl**, *Supply Chain Management: Strategy, Planning, and Operation*.
- All three macro processes (CRM, ISCM, and SRM) must share a unified data platform; siloed execution between them causes severe planning mismatches.
