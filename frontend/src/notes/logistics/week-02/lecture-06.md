# Week 2 – Lecture 6: Tools for Improving Logistics Efficiency (Contd.)

## 1. Key Concepts
- **The 7 Quality Control (QC) Tools:** A foundational suite of graphical and statistical techniques codified by Kaoru Ishikawa to diagnose logistics errors, identify root causes, and monitor process stability.
- **5S Methodology:** A Japanese workplace organization framework aimed at creating clean, visual, standardized, and safe warehouse environments.
- **Benchmarking:** The continuous process of measuring logistics practices, cycle times, and operational costs against recognized industry leaders or world-class competitors.

## 2. Important Points
- **The Seven QC Tools in Logistics:**
  1. *Cause and Effect Diagram (Ishikawa / Fishbone):* Identifies potential root causes across 6 categories (Man, Machine, Material, Method, Measurement, Environment) leading to logistics failures like late delivery.
  2. *Check Sheet:* Standardized forms for real-time manual data capture at receipt and dispatch docks.
  3. *Control Chart:* Monitors whether order picking errors or delivery turnaround times remain within statistical limits ($CL \pm 3\sigma$).
  4. *Histogram:* Shows distribution shape of transit lead times.
  5. *Pareto Chart:* Identifies the top $20\%$ of delivery defect categories causing $80\%$ of customer claims.
  6. *Scatter Diagram:* Tests correlation between variables (e.g., driver route distance vs. fuel consumption).
  7. *Flowchart / Process Map:* Visualizes material and documentation workflows to spot delays.
- **The 5S Framework:**
  - **Seiri (Sort):** Segregate and remove unnecessary broken pallets, obsolete stock, and clutter.
  - **Seiton (Set in order):** Designate clear, labeled physical locations for all inventory, pallet jacks, and tools ("A place for everything and everything in its place").
  - **Seiso (Shine):** Clean floors, racks, and vehicles daily to expose oil leaks or damaged equipment.
  - **Seiketsu (Standardize):** Formalize checklists, visual signs, and color-coded lane markings.
  - **Shitsuke (Sustain):** Foster self-discipline and conduct regular audits to maintain standards.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Tool | Core Function in Logistics | Practical Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ishikawa Diagram** | Brainstorms all possible failure causes | Visual fishbone diagram of delayed shipment causes |
| **Pareto Chart** | Prioritizes the most critical failure modes | Bar chart sorting warehouse damage types by frequency |
| **5S Framework** | Physical warehouse floor organization | Clean, organized aisles with zero trip hazards and marked bays |

## 4. Must Remember
- Developer of the 7 QC tools: **Prof. Kaoru Ishikawa**.
- 5S is not merely industrial housekeeping; it directly reduces order picking travel time and eliminates workplace logistics accidents.
