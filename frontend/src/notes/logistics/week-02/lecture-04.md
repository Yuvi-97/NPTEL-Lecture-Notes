# Week 2 – Lecture 4: Logistics Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Logistics Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Quantitative metrics utilized to monitor, measure, and evaluate operational efficiency, customer service quality, and financial health across logistics functions.
- **On-Time In-Full (OTIF):** A premier customer fulfillment metric measuring the percentage of customer orders delivered strictly on schedule with complete item quantities.
- **Perfect Order Index (POI):** The compound probability that an order completes every stage of the fulfillment cycle flawlessly.

## 2. Important Points
- **Functional Logistics KPIs Breakdown:**
  - **Transportation KPIs:**
    - Freight cost per unit / ton-kilometer.
    - Truck turnaround time at loading bays.
    - Vehicle cubic/weight capacity utilization rate.
    - Transit time variance / on-time delivery rate.
  - **Warehousing KPIs:**
    - Inventory Turnover Ratio ($\text{ITR} = \frac{\text{Cost of Goods Sold}}{\text{Average Inventory}}$).
    - Order picking accuracy percentage ($> 99.5\%$).
    - Dock-to-stock cycle time (hours to unpack, inspect, and shelf incoming goods).
    - Storage space utilization percentage.
  - **Order Management & Customer Service:**
    - Order Lead Time (order placement to delivery).
    - Order Fill Rate (line-item fulfillment percentage).
    - Perfect Order Index formulation:
      $$\text{POI} = \% \text{On-Time} \times \% \text{In-Full} \times \% \text{Damage-Free} \times \% \text{Accurate Invoicing}$$

## 3. Differences / Examples
| KPI Category | Focus | Example Metric | Target Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operational Efficiency** | Internal resource utilization | Vehicle capacity utilization | Higher is better |
| **Service Quality** | Customer fulfillment satisfaction | Perfect Order Index (POI) | Higher is better ($> 95\%$) |
| **Cost Performance** | Financial expenditure | Logistics cost as $\%$ of sales | Lower is better ($< 8\text{–}10\%$) |

## 4. Must Remember
- Total Logistics Cost generally consumes **$8\text{–}14\%$ of corporate revenue**; monitoring functional KPIs directly protects operating profit margins.
- POI is a multiplicative compound metric: if four individual stages perform at $95\%$ each, the true Perfect Order rate drops to $0.95^4 \approx 81.4\%$.
