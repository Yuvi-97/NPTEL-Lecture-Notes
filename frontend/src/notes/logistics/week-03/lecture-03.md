# Week 3 – Lecture 3: Green Logistics

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Green Logistics:** The systematic measurement, assessment, and minimization of the ecological and environmental footprint of all logistics and freight operations.
- **Environmental Externalities:** Unpriced ecological damages generated by logistics activities, including greenhouse gas emissions ($CO_2, CH_4$), air pollution ($NO_x, SO_x$, particulate matter), traffic congestion, and noise pollution.
- **Triple Bottom Line in Sustainable Supply Chains:** Harmonizing three operational pillars: **Economic** (Profit), **Environmental** (Planet), and **Social** (People).

## 2. Important Points
- **Core Dimensions of Green Logistics:**
  - **Modal Shift (Intermodal Transport):** Shifting long-haul freight from high-emission road trucking to low-carbon rail or coastal/inland water shipping:
    - Waterway/Rail emits approximately $70\text{–}80\%$ less $CO_2$ per ton-kilometer than diesel trucks.
  - **Green Fleet Management:** Adopting electric vehicles (EVs), compressed natural gas (CNG), hybrid drivetrains, aerodynamic truck fairings, and telematics to cut fuel idling.
  - **Green Warehousing:** Installing rooftop solar photovoltaic arrays, energy-efficient LED sensory lighting, rainwater harvesting, and electric forklifts.
  - **Eco-Packaging:** Transitioning from non-recyclable expanded polystyrene and single-use bubble wrap to biodegradable corrugated cardboard and reusable returnable plastic totes.
  - **Network & Route Optimization:** Using algorithmic load consolidation to eliminate empty backhauls and shorten delivery mileage.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Term | Primary Operational Focus | Core Driving Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reverse Logistics** | Moving products backward for value recapture | Net salvage recovery value & return cycle time |
| **Green Logistics** | Minimizing environmental impact and pollution | Carbon footprint ($g CO_2e / \text{ton-km}$), packaging waste |
| **Sustainable SCM** | Holistic environmental, economic, and social stewardship | Comprehensive ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) audit scores |

## 4. Must Remember
- Transportation generates over **$20\text{–}25\%$ of global carbon emissions**, with freight transport representing a rapidly expanding share.
- Green logistics initiatives frequently yield direct financial cost savings through reduced fuel consumption and lighter packaging materials.
