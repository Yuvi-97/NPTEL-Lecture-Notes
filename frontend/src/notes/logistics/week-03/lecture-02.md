# Week 3 – Lecture 2: Reverse Logistics (Contd.)

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Gatekeeping:** The critical initial screening process occurring at the entry point of the reverse logistics chain to inspect, verify, and decide whether a returned product should be accepted into the return stream.
- **Disposition:** The strategic operational decision determining the specific value-recovery or disposal path for an accepted returned item.
- **Secondary Markets:** Alternative discounted resale channels (e.g., outlet stores, liquidators, factory seconds platforms) used to monetize surplus and returned inventory.

## 2. Important Points
- **The Five-Step Reverse Logistics Process:**
  1. *Collection & Consolidation:* Gathering returned items from retail points or customer doorsteps at regional return hubs.
  2. *Gatekeeping & Inspection:* Scanning barcodes, verifying return authorization, and grading physical condition (Brand new, Damaged, Defective).
  3. *Disposition Decision:* Assigning products to one of the recovery hierarchies:
     - **Resell As-Is:** Re-box and return to primary inventory.
     - **Refurbish / Repair:** Replace minor worn parts and clean for secondary sale.
     - **Remanufacture:** Disassemble and rebuild to original factory performance standards.
     - **Cannibalize:** Harvest functioning sub-components for warranty repairs.
     - **Recycle:** Extract raw base materials (metals, plastics, glass).
     - **Dispose / Incinerate:** Secure disposal in compliance with environmental laws (last resort).
  4. *Processing & Reconditioning:* Executing the chosen disposition pathway.
  5. *Redistribution:* Shipping salvaged products to secondary markets.
- **Key Reverse Logistics KPIs:** Return processing cycle time, recovery percentage value, customer refund turnaround time, and landfill diversion rate.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Disposition Option | Processing Effort | Value Recaptured | Warranty Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Resell As-Is** | Minimal (re-packaging) | $90\text{–}100\%$ original retail | Full original warranty |
| **Refurbish** | Moderate (repairs & testing) | $50\text{–}70\%$ original retail | Limited 90-day warranty |
| **Recycle** | Extensive (material shredding) | $5\text{–}15\%$ scrap raw material value | No warranty |

## 4. Must Remember
- Strict **Gatekeeping** at the point of customer collection is the most effective lever for controlling reverse logistics costs by rejecting fraudulent or ineligible returns early.
- Remanufacturing uses up to $85\%$ less energy than manufacturing brand-new products from virgin raw materials.
