# Week 3 – Lecture 6: Lean Logistics: Lean Six Sigma & DMAIC

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Lean Six Sigma (LSS) in Logistics:** An integrated business excellence methodology combining the speed and waste-elimination focus of **Lean** with the quality variance reduction and data-driven discipline of **Six Sigma**.
- **Six Sigma Standard:** An operational quality benchmark representing virtually defect-free performance, permitting no more than **3.4 Defects Per Million Opportunities (DPMO)** ($99.99966\%$ accuracy).
- **DMAIC Methodology:** A five-phase structured problem-solving cycle: **Define**, **Measure**, **Analyze**, **Improve**, and **Control**.

## 2. Important Points
- **The DMAIC Process Applied to Logistics:**
  - **Define:** Develop the project charter; identify the Voice of Customer (VOC) and translate it into Critical to Quality (CTQ) parameters (e.g., reduce freight damage below $0.1\%$).
  - **Measure:** Map current process performance; collect baseline delivery data; validate measurement systems (Gage R&R).
  - **Analyze:** Identify root causes of logistics variability using Fishbone diagrams, Pareto analysis, and hypothesis testing.
  - **Improve:** Formulate and pilot targeted interventions (e.g., redesign protective corner boards, implement Poka-Yoke barcode scan interlocks before pallet strapping).
  - **Control:** Institutionalize gains using Statistical Process Control (SPC) charts, standardized work instructions, and weekly visual management audits.
- **Poka-Yoke (Mistake Proofing):** Physical or digital mechanisms that make human operational errors impossible (e.g., warehouse pick-to-light systems requiring the worker to scan the exact bin before the cart opens).

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Dimension | Lean Methodology | Six Sigma Methodology | Lean Six Sigma Synergy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Objective** | Eliminate non-value-adding waste | Reduce process variance & defects | Fast velocity AND defect-free deliveries |
| **Primary Focus** | Velocity, flow, lead time | Quality, consistency, accuracy | Complete end-to-end supply chain perfection |
| **Primary Metric** | Cycle time, inventory turnover | Defect rate, standard deviation ($\sigma$) | OTIF & Perfect Order Index (POI) |

## 4. Must Remember
- Lean without Six Sigma results in fast, streamlined processes that still produce errors; Six Sigma without Lean creates high-quality processes that remain sluggish and bloated.
- Six Sigma defines perfection as **$\le 3.4$ errors per million shipments or picks**.
