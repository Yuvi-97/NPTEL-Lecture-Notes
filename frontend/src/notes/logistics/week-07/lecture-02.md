# Week 7 – Lecture 2: Strategic Supply Chain Management

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Strategic Supply Chain Management:** The long-term alignment of supply chain capabilities, organizational structure, and partner alliances with the overarching corporate business strategy to achieve sustainable competitive advantage.
- **Strategic Intent:** The clear articulation of an organization's future direction, incorporating its **Vision** (what it aspires to become), **Mission** (its core operational purpose), and **Core Competencies**.
- **Three Strategic Levels:** Corporate Strategy (defining what industries/markets to contest), Business Strategy (how to compete in a specific market), and Functional Strategy (how the supply chain delivers that advantage).

## 2. Important Points
- **The Five-Stage Strategic Management Process:**
  1. *Strategic Intent Definition:* Establishing the organization’s vision, mission, and long-range goals.
  2. *Environmental Scanning:* Comprehensive assessment of external macro-market threats/opportunities and internal operational strengths/weaknesses.
  3. *Strategy Formulation:* Selecting competitive postures (e.g., Cost Leadership vs. Differentiation vs. Focus) and configuring supply chain networks accordingly.
  4. *Strategy Implementation:* Mobilizing physical assets, assigning capital budgets, structuring vendor partnerships, and implementing enterprise software (ERP, WMS).
  5. *Strategic Evaluation & Control:* Monitoring performance scorecards, tracking KPI variances, and executing corrective strategic adjustments.
- **Key Authors:** References Hitt, Ireland, & Hoskisson (*Strategic Management*); Michael Porter (*Competitive Strategy*).

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Strategic Level | Primary Decision Maker | Scope of Decisions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Corporate Strategy** | Board of Directors / CEO | Diversification, acquisitions, entry into new global markets |
| **Business Strategy** | Business Unit Heads | Cost leadership vs. Product differentiation in a specific sector |
| **Supply Chain Strategy** | Chief Supply Chain Officer (CSCO) | Network design, sourcing policy, inventory placement, logistics modes |

## 4. Must Remember
- A supply chain strategy cannot exist in a vacuum: it must be derived directly from the broader **business competitive strategy**.
- Strategy formulation is theoretical; the true test of strategic management lies in disciplined **implementation and real-time operational control**.
