# Week 7 – Lecture 6: Make or Buy Decision

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Make-or-Buy Decision:** The strategic managerial determination of whether an enterprise should manufacture a component or perform a logistics service internally in-house (**Make**) or contract an external third-party supplier (**Buy / Outsource**).
- **Core Competencies (C.K. Prahalad & Gary Hamel):** The unique bundle of proprietary skills, technologies, and intellectual property that delivers distinctive customer value and cannot be easily replicated by competitors.
- **Strategic Alliances:** Cooperative partnerships between independent firms to share capabilities and risk, categorized into **Vertical Alliances** (supplier-buyer links) and **Horizontal Alliances** (peer-to-peer or competitor collaboration).

## 2. Important Points
- **Quantitative Break-Even Analysis:**
  - Let $FC$ = In-house Fixed Cost, $VC$ = In-house Variable Cost per unit, and $P$ = External Supplier Purchase Price per unit ($P > VC$).
  - Total In-house Cost: $TC_{\text{Make}} = FC + (VC \times Q)$
  - Total Buy Cost: $TC_{\text{Buy}} = P \times Q$
  - **Break-Even Quantity ($Q_{\text{BE}}$):**
    $$Q_{\text{BE}} = \frac{FC}{P - VC}$$
  - If forecasted demand $Q > Q_{\text{BE}}$: **Make in-house** (spread fixed costs over high volume).
  - If forecasted demand $Q < Q_{\text{BE}}$: **Buy / Outsource** (avoid heavy fixed capital investment).
- **Qualitative Decision Factors:**
  - Protect core intellectual property and proprietary trade secrets (favor Make).
  - Lack of internal technical expertise or capital investment funds (favor Buy).
  - Supplier dependency and risk of supplier opportunism (Transaction Cost Economics - Oliver Williamson).

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Strategic Option | Major Advantages | Major Risks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Make (In-House)** | Total operational control, IP protection, design confidentiality | Heavy capital expenditure, risk of obsolete technology |
| **Buy (Outsource)** | Lower capital investment, vendor specialized economies of scale | Loss of internal technical skills, supplier quality risk |

## 4. Must Remember
- **Golden Rule of Outsourcing:** Outsource commodity and non-core operational activities; **never outsource core competencies**.
- Quantitative break-even analysis provides the baseline financial threshold, but qualitative factors (quality, IP risk, supplier stability) govern final strategic decisions.
