# Week 7 – Lecture 3: Strategic Supply Chain Management (Contd.)

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Environmental Scanning in SCM:** The structured surveillance and analysis of external macro-environmental forces, industry competitive dynamics, and internal enterprise resources to inform supply chain strategy.
- **PESTEL Analysis:** An analytical tool auditing six macro-environmental dimensions: **P**olitical, **E**conomic, **S**ocial, **T**echnological, **E**nvironmental, and **L**egal.
- **Porter’s Five Forces Model:** An industrial organization framework that diagnoses the structural intensity of market competition and profit attractiveness.

## 2. Important Points
- **PESTEL Dimensions Applied to Supply Chains:**
  - *Political:* Trade tariffs, cross-border border controls, trade embargoes.
  - *Economic:* Fuel prices, currency exchange rates, inflation, labor wage rates.
  - *Social:* Urbanization trends, aging workforce, green consumer preferences.
  - *Technological:* Blockchain tracking, autonomous transport, 5G IoT telemetry.
  - *Environmental:* Carbon emission mandates, plastic packaging phase-outs.
  - *Legal:* Labor laws, import quotas, maritime cabotage regulations.
- **Porter’s Five Forces in Supply Chain Networks:**
  1. *Bargaining Power of Suppliers:* High when suppliers are few and switching costs are high.
  2. *Bargaining Power of Buyers:* High when customers demand short lead times and free delivery.
  3. *Threat of New Entrants:* Low when distribution network scale barriers and capital costs are high.
  4. *Threat of Substitute Products:* Disruptive alternatives (e.g., 3D printing replacing spare parts freight).
  5. *Industry Rivalry:* Price wars and service competition among competing supply chains.
- **SWOT Synthesis:** Synthesizing internal **S**trengths and **W**eaknesses with external **O**pportunities and **T**hreats.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Analytical Framework | Environment Analyzed | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PESTEL** | Macro-environment | Broad societal, technological, and geopolitical trends |
| **Porter’s Five Forces** | Micro-industry environment | Competitive rivalry and profit power dynamics between suppliers, firms, and buyers |
| **SWOT** | Internal & External interface | Matching internal operational strengths against external market opportunities |

## 4. Must Remember
- Competition in the 21st century is no longer between individual standalone companies; it is **between competing supply chains**.
- High supplier bargaining power severely squeezes enterprise margins, requiring defensive dual-sourcing or vertical integration strategies.
