# Week 8 – Lecture 5: Supply Chain Performance: Balanced Scorecard Approach (Contd.)

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Supply Chain Strategy Map:** A visual diagram depicting the explicit cause-and-effect hypothesis linking intangible asset investments in Learning & Growth up to ultimate corporate Financial profitability.
- **Cascading Balanced Scorecards:** The process of decomposing high-level enterprise supply chain objectives down to regional distribution centers, transport fleets, and individual warehouse shift teams.
- **KPI Dashboards:** Visual management consoles presenting real-time operational metrics against predetermined performance thresholds (Red-Amber-Green / RAG status).

## 2. Important Points
- **The Cause-and-Effect Chain (The Strategy Map Engine):**
  1. *Learning & Growth:* Investment in employee digital training and IoT inventory telemetry...
  2. $\to$ *Drives Internal Business Processes:* Reduces warehouse picking errors, accelerates dock-to-stock cycle times, and streamlines supplier collaboration...
  3. $\to$ *Drives Customer Satisfaction:* Delivers orders on-time, in-full, and without damage, shortening customer lead times...
  4. $\to$ *Drives Financial Results:* Generates repeat customer business, eliminates waste and scrap write-offs, accelerates cash-to-cash velocity, and expands operating margins.
- **Balanced Performance Indices:**
  - *Quality Performance Index (QPI):* Composite metric tracking incoming material quality, picking accuracy, and transit damage.
  - *Delivery Performance Index (DPI):* Measures on-time dispatch and transit lead time compliance.
  - *Cost Performance Index (CPI):* Ratio of budgeted supply chain expenditure to actual operating spend.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Scorecard Level | User Level | Focus & Granularity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise Supply Chain BSC** | Chief Supply Chain Officer (CSCO) | Global network margin, total landed cost, overall OTIF |
| **Facility / Plant BSC** | Warehouse / Factory Manager | Dock turnaround time, pick rate per man-hour, local inventory turns |
| **Frontline Team BSC** | Shift Supervisor / Team Leader | Daily line throughput, shift picking accuracy, safety near-misses |

## 4. Must Remember
- The true power of the Balanced Scorecard is its **cause-and-effect linkage**: every operational measure must logically connect to customer satisfaction and financial profitability.
- Scorecard targets must be ambitious yet achievable; disconnected KPIs lead to local gaming rather than systemic improvement.
