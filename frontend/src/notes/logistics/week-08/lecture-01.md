# Week 8 – Lecture 1: Strategic Sourcing

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Strategic Sourcing:** A systematic, long-range procurement methodology that manages supplier relationships as collaborative enterprise assets rather than short-term transactional buying.
- **The Kraljic Portfolio Purchasing Matrix (Peter Kraljic, 1983):** A landmark procurement framework classifying purchased materials into four quadrants based on **Profit Impact** and **Supply Risk**.
- **Supplier Relationship Management (SRM):** Segmenting the vendor base to focus managerial bandwidth and strategic capital where supplier failure poses the greatest risk.

## 2. Important Points
- **The Four Quadrants of the Kraljic Matrix:**
  1. **Non-Critical / Routine Items (Low Profit Impact, Low Supply Risk):**
     - Standardized catalog items (e.g., office supplies, standard fasteners).
     - *Strategy:* Streamline procurement, automate order processing, use e-procurement catalogs to minimize administrative overhead.
  2. **Leverage Items (High Profit Impact, Low Supply Risk):**
     - Commodity goods purchased in huge volumes with abundant suppliers (e.g., bulk steel, basic packaging cartons).
     - *Strategy:* Exploit purchasing power, reverse auctions, competitive bidding, volume consolidation across business units.
  3. **Bottleneck Items (Low Profit Impact, High Supply Risk):**
     - Specialized niche components with few qualified producers (e.g., proprietary spare parts, specialized catalysts).
     - *Strategy:* Secure long-term supply contracts, hold safety inventory buffers, develop alternative specifications.
  4. **Strategic Items (High Profit Impact, High Supply Risk):**
     - High-value, critical custom components defining core product functionality (e.g., custom microprocessors, proprietary engines).
     - *Strategy:* Establish deep strategic partnerships, joint product development, open-book cost accounting, and shared risk.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Kraljic Quadrant | Profit Impact | Supply Risk | Primary Strategic Procurement Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Non-Critical** | Low | Low | Product standardization & process automation |
| **Leverage** | High | Low | Aggressive price negotiation & volume bundling |
| **Bottleneck** | Low | High | Ensure supply continuity & build buffer stock |
| **Strategic** | High | High | Form close collaborative alliances & long-term contracts |

## 4. Must Remember
- Reference text: **Peter Kraljic (1983)**, "Purchasing Must Become Supply Management", *Harvard Business Review*.
- A company should never treat all vendors identically: strategic items require collaborative trust, while leverage items require aggressive market exploitation.
