# Week 8 – Lecture 2: Vendor Rating

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Vendor Rating:** The formal, quantitative assessment and continuous evaluation of an approved supplier’s operational performance against predefined, objective performance criteria.
- **Supplier Development:** Utilizing vendor evaluation scorecards to identify operational bottlenecks and assist suppliers in upgrading their quality, delivery, and technological capabilities.
- **Total Acquisition Cost:** Evaluating vendors not merely on initial quoted unit invoice price, but on total landed cost including defect scrap, delivery delays, and warranty claims.

## 2. Important Points
- **Core Vendor Rating Criteria:**
  - *Quality:* Incoming defect rate, Parts Per Million (PPM) non-conforming, scrap and rework percentage.
  - *Delivery Reliability:* On-Time Delivery (OTD), compliance with agreed shipment quantities, transit lead time consistency.
  - *Cost Competitiveness:* Quoted price, willingness to share cost breakdowns, payment term flexibility.
  - *Service & Technical Capability:* Responsiveness to emergency orders, design support, warranty resolution.
- **Major Vendor Rating Methodologies:**
  1. **Categorical Method:** Qualitative grading (e.g., Preferred, Approved, Conditional, De-listed) evaluated quarterly by a cross-functional procurement and engineering committee.
  2. **Weighted Point Method:** The most widely adopted quantitative system:
     - Assign percentage weights ($w_i$) to key criteria ($\sum w_i = 100\%$, e.g., Quality = $40\%$, Delivery = $30\%$, Price = $20\%$, Service = $10\%$).
     - Calculate composite weighted vendor score:
       $$\text{Vendor Score} = \sum_{i=1}^n (w_i \times \text{Rating}_i)$$
  3. **Cost-Ratio Method:** Quantifies non-performance costs (e.g., inspection, late delivery fines) as a percentage penalty added to the supplier’s base quoted price.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Evaluation Method | Complexity | Objectivity | Operational Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Categorical Method** | Low | Subjective (expert consensus) | Very Low |
| **Weighted Point Method** | Moderate | Objective (data-driven KPI scores) | Moderate |
| **Cost-Ratio Method** | High | Highly precise (direct financial penalty) | High (requires detailed activity-based accounting) |

## 4. Must Remember
- The true purpose of vendor rating is **supplier continuous improvement and development**, not merely punitive disqualification.
- A supplier with the lowest unit price can end up being the most expensive supplier if poor quality leads to factory line stoppages.
