# Week 11 – Lecture 5: Safety Performance Indicators Part-II

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Process Safety Indicators:** Quantitative metrics tailored specifically to high-hazard chemical and petrochemical handling facilities to evaluate the integrity of primary containment barriers.
- **Offshore Dockside & Chemical Terminal Case Study:** Traces hazardous fluid transfer from marine cargo ships through unloading gantries, overland pipelines, bulk storage tank farms, and final road tanker loading bays.
- **Asset Integrity KPIs:** Performance metrics ensuring physical barriers (piping walls, valves, relief systems) do not degrade over their operating lifecycle.

## 2. Important Points
- **Hazard Scenarios Evaluated in Case Study:**
  1. *Transfer Pipeline Rupture:* Loss of hazardous fluid containment due to corrosion, pressure surges, or mechanical impact.
  2. *Bulk Storage Tank Failure:* Overfill, catastrophic tank shell rupture, or vapor explosion.
  3. *Road Tanker Loading Mishap:* Static spark ignition, hose detachment, or overpressure release.
- **Specific Leading KPIs for Chemical Terminals:**
  - *Pipeline:* Percentage of ultrasonic wall-thickness inspections completed on schedule; annual corrosion rate compliance.
  - *Storage Tanks:* High-level emergency trip switch test completion rate; bund wall containment integrity inspection frequency.
  - *Loading Gantries:* Earth continuity interlock test compliance; dry-disconnect coupling maintenance adherence.
- **Specific Lagging KPIs for Chemical Terminals:**
  - Loss of Primary Containment (LOPC) frequency (Tier 1 and Tier 2 process safety events under API RP 754).
  - Minor chemical spill volumes ($< 50 \text{ kg}$) contained within bunded areas.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Terminal Asset | Major Hazard | Key Leading KPI | Key Lagging KPI |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transfer Pipeline** | Hydrocarbon leak / rupture | Cathodic protection potential check compliance | Uncontained pipe pinhole leaks |
| **Bulk Storage Tank** | Overfill / Vapor cloud | High-level alarm & trip calibration frequency | Tank overflow / secondary bund breach |
| **Road Tanker Bay** | Static ignition during filling | Grounding clamp interlock check compliance | Flash fires or emergency hose disconnections |

## 4. Must Remember
- Industrial case study emphasizes: **Bund walls / secondary dikes** must always be maintained empty of rainwater to ensure full retention capacity during a bulk tank rupture.
- Process safety indicators must track both primary containment barriers and secondary emergency mitigation barriers.
