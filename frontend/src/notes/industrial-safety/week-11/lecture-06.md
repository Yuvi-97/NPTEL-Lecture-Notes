# Week 11 – Lecture 6: Energy Isolations

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Energy Isolation:** The systematic procedure of physically disconnecting and securing all hazardous energy sources supplying a machine or system before personnel perform maintenance, servicing, or internal inspection.
- **Lockout / Tagout (LOTO):** The primary engineered administrative control system ensuring equipment cannot be inadvertently energized while workers are exposed.
- **Zero Energy State:** The complete elimination and dissipation of all residual, stored, or secondary energy in a machine, rendering it completely inert and safe.

## 2. Important Points
- **Forms of Hazardous Energy in Industry:**
  - *Electrical:* High/low voltage switchgear, motor drives, capacitor banks.
  - *Hydraulic:* High-pressure oil lines, hydraulic accumulators, fluid rams.
  - *Pneumatic:* Compressed air lines, pressurized gas receivers.
  - *Chemical:* Trapped toxic, corrosive, or flammable gases and liquids in piping.
  - *Thermal:* Steam lines, hot surfaces, cryogenic vessels.
  - *Mechanical / Gravity:* Suspended loads, mechanical tension springs, flywheels.
- **The Six Essential Steps of Energy Isolation:**
  1. **Preparation:** Identify all incoming and stored energy sources.
  2. **Shutdown:** Orderly shutdown of equipment using standard operational controls.
  3. **Isolation:** Physically disconnect breakers, close and chain isolating valves, install blind flanges.
  4. **Lockout & Tagout:** Attach individual personal safety padlocks and danger identification tags to each isolation point.
  5. **Dissipation (Stored Energy Release):** Bleed trapped fluid/pneumatic pressure, ground electrical capacitors, insert mechanical chocks beneath suspended components.
  6. **Verification ("Try" Step):** Test operational start switches and use voltmeters/pressure gauges to prove zero energy exists before beginning work.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Component | Lockout Device | Tagout Device |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Mechanism** | Heavy-duty key-operated padlock or physical lockout hasp | Standardized weather-resistant visual warning label |
| **Protection Type** | Physical positive restraint (prevents physical movement/switch-on) | Informational warning only (identifies worker, contact, and date) |
| **Security Rule** | "One Person, One Lock, One Key" (no master key in possession of others) | Never used alone when a physical lock can be installed |

## 4. Must Remember
- The **"Verification / Try Step"** (Step 6) is the single most vital life-saving step in the LOTO protocol: never assume isolation without physical testing.
- A single breaker switch does not isolate stored hydraulic or pneumatic energy: all accumulators must be bled down to atmospheric pressure.
