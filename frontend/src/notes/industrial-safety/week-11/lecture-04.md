# Week 11 – Lecture 4: Safety Performance Indicators Part-I

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Safety Performance Indicators (SPIs):** Measurable parameters used by organizations to track, assess, and manage health and safety performance against predetermined objectives.
- **"What gets measured gets managed":** The core principle that continuous safety improvement is impossible without reliable quantitative metrics.
- **Lagging vs. Leading Indicators:**
  - *Lagging Indicators (Reactive):* Metrics that quantify past safety failures, injuries, and system damage after an event has already occurred.
  - *Leading Indicators (Proactive):* Predictive operational metrics that measure safety activities, risk barriers, and procedural compliance before accidents happen.

## 2. Important Points
- **Common Lagging Indicators:**
  - **Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR):** Number of lost-time injuries per million (or 200,000) employee-hours worked:
    $$\text{LTIFR} = \frac{\text{Number of Lost Time Injuries} \times 1,000,000}{\text{Total Man-Hours Worked}}$$
  - **Total Recordable Injury Rate (TRIR):** Encompasses fatalities, lost-time injuries, restricted work cases, and medical treatment cases.
  - **Severity Rate:** Lost workdays per thousand hours worked.
- **Common Leading Indicators:**
  - Number of Near-Miss reports logged and resolved.
  - Percentage of scheduled preventive maintenance tasks completed on time.
  - Percentage of workforce completing mandated safety refresher training.
  - Number of leadership safety walk-throughs conducted per month.
  - Audit closure rate for identified safety non-conformances.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Metric Dimension | Lagging Indicators | Leading Indicators |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Orientation** | Reactive (historical outcome) | Proactive (predictive input/process) |
| **Measurement Subject** | Harm, injuries, asset loss | Barrier health, training, audits, inspections |
| **Limitation** | Provides zero insight into imminent risks if no incident has occurred yet | Requires disciplined reporting and valid data capture |
| **Ideal Role** | Demonstrates bottom-line historical progress | Provides early warning signals to avert impending disasters |

## 4. Must Remember
- Relying exclusively on lagging indicators creates a false sense of security: a facility can have zero injuries for months while latent mechanical barriers are on the verge of catastrophic failure.
- A balanced safety dashboard combines **both leading and lagging indicators**.
