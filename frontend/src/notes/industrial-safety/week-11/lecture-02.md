# Week 11 – Lecture 2: OH&SMS and OHSAS 18001-Part II

## 1. Key Concepts
- **PDCA Cycle (Deming Cycle):** The fundamental iterative management loop underpinning OHSAS 18001 / ISO 45001, comprising **Plan**, **Do**, **Check**, and **Act**.
- **Continual Improvement:** The ongoing process of enhancing the OH&SMS to achieve improvements in overall occupational health and safety performance aligned with the organization's safety policy.
- **Corporate Safety Policy:** A formal statement authored and signed by top executive management declaring organizational commitment to hazard elimination, injury prevention, and legal compliance.

## 2. Important Points
- **The Four Phases of OHSAS 18001:**
  1. **Plan:**
     - Establish corporate OHS policy.
     - Identify hazards, assess risks, and determine control measures (HIRA).
     - Maintain legal and statutory compliance registers.
     - Formulate measurable safety objectives and operational management programs.
  2. **Do (Implementation & Operation):**
     - Define roles, accountability, and organizational structure.
     - Deliver worker competency, training, and risk awareness.
     - Facilitate internal/external communication and worker consultation.
     - Establish operational controls and emergency preparedness/response plans.
  3. **Check (Measurement & Evaluation):**
     - Monitor performance (routine inspections, safety audits).
     - Investigate accidents, incidents, and near-misses.
     - Implement Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) for non-conformities.
     - Conduct periodic formal internal audits.
  4. **Act (Management Review):**
     - Executive leadership reviews audit results, incident trends, and resource adequacy to drive continuous improvement.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| PDCA Phase | Core Objective | Key Operational Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plan** | Establish objectives & processes | Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment (HIRA) register |
| **Do** | Execute planned controls | Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), training logs, work permits |
| **Check** | Verify compliance & progress | Internal audit reports, incident investigation findings |
| **Act** | Close gaps & drive improvement | Executive management review decisions & resource allocations |

## 4. Must Remember
- OHSAS 18001 operates as a continuous closed-loop: without executive **Act** (Management Review), the system degenerates into static paperwork.
- Top management commitment is the single most critical success factor for an effective OH&SMS.
