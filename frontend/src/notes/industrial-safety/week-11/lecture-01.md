# Week 11 – Lecture 1: OH&SMS and OHSAS 18001-Part I

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Occupational Health & Safety Management System (OH&SMS):** A structured, organization-wide framework of policies, procedures, and responsibilities established to proactively manage workplace health and safety risks.
- **OHSAS 18001:** An internationally recognized standard for occupational health and safety management systems (now evolved into ISO 45001), developed to assist organizations in demonstrating robust safety governance.
- **OSHA vs. OHSAS Distinction:**
  - *OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration):* A federal regulatory enforcement agency under the United States Department of Labor.
  - *OHSAS 18001:* A voluntary international management system specification applicable worldwide.

## 2. Important Points
- **Three Eras of Industrial Safety Evolution:**
  1. *Technical Phase (Early 1900s–1970s):* Focus on machine guarding, structural integrity, and mechanical failure reduction.
  2. *Human Factors Phase (1970s–1990s):* Focus on ergonomics, individual behavior, human error, and training.
  3. *Organizational / Systems Phase (1990s–Present):* Focus on corporate safety culture, leadership commitment, management systems (OH&SMS), and holistic operational governance.
- **Business-Integrated Safety:** Modern safety management is not an isolated policing task; it is fundamentally integrated into production operations, quality control, cost management, and business continuity.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Organization / Standard | Geographic Scope | Legal Nature | Core Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OSHA** | United States national jurisdiction | Statutory / Regulatory law | Enforcing mandatory federal workplace safety standards |
| **OHSAS 18001 / ISO 45001** | Global international application | Voluntary management framework | Certifying management processes for continuous risk reduction |
