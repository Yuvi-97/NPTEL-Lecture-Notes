# Week 10 – Lecture 1: Accident Investigation

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Accident Investigation:** A systematic, structured examination of an undesirable workplace event designed to determine why and how it happened, identify underlying systemic root causes, and prescribe corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) to prevent recurrence.
- **Root Cause Analysis (RCA):** Methods aimed at identifying fundamental organizational, human, and engineering breakdowns rather than merely addressing superficial immediate symptoms.
- **Blameless Culture:** The fundamental investigative principle that safety investigations seek facts to fix systems, not to assign individual punitive culpability.

## 2. Important Points
- **The Six Stages of Incident Investigation:**
  1. *Immediate Action:* Provide first aid, isolate energy sources, and secure the physical scene.
  2. *Data Collection:* Gather physical evidence, examine maintenance/log records, and conduct prompt witness interviews.
  3. *Sequence Reconstruction:* Assemble a chronological timeline of events leading up to and following the mishap.
  4. *Analysis (RCA):* Deploy structured techniques such as 5-Whys, Ishikawa (Fishbone) diagrams, or TapRooT.
  5. *Recommendations:* Formulate SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) corrective interventions.
  6. *Reporting & Follow-up:* Distribute the formal investigation report and track action item close-out.
- **Multidisciplinary Investigation Team:** Should comprise safety professionals, frontline operators, technical domain engineers, maintenance supervisors, and safety committee representatives.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Element | Immediate Cause | Root Cause |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nature** | Direct physical symptom (e.g., worker slipped on oil pool) | Systemic organizational failure (e.g., defective pump seal inspection policy) |
| **Intervention** | Mop the floor | Overhaul preventive maintenance schedule & install fluid containment |
| **Recurrence Risk** | High (accident will repeat when oil spills again) | Low (permanently resolves failure mechanism) |

## 4. Must Remember
- The primary objective of accident investigation is **prevention of recurrence**, never fault-finding or punishment.
- Evidence collection must occur immediately before the physical scene is disturbed or human memory degrades.
