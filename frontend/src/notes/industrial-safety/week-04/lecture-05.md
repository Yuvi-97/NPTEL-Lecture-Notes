# Week 4 – Lecture 5: Bow-Tie: Identification of Safety Barriers

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Safety Barriers:** Physical, engineered, administrative, or human actions deployed across a bow-tie model to interrupt accident propagation paths.
- **Preventive vs. Mitigative Barriers:**
  - *Preventive Barriers:* Positioned left of the center event to prevent hazards or threats from escalating into the Top Event.
  - *Mitigative Barriers:* Positioned right of the center event to arrest consequence propagation and protect lives, assets, and the environment.
- **Barrier Quality Dimensions:** Independence, effectiveness, reliability, and auditability.

## 2. Important Points
- **Barrier Classification Types:**
  1. **Technical / Hardware Barriers:** Physical containment, interlocks, pressure relief valves, safety nets, guardrails.
  2. **Organizational / Administrative Barriers:** Operating procedures, permit-to-work systems, routine inspection checklists.
  3. **Behavioral / Human Barriers:** PPE compliance, situational awareness, correct operator emergency actions.
- **Case Examples Examined:**
  - **Fall from Fixed Scaffold:**
    - *Top Event:* Worker falling from scaffold platform.
    - *Preventive Barriers:* Standard guardrails, toe boards, secure planking, harness tie-off points.
    - *Mitigative Barriers:* Safety catch nets, deceleration arrestors, on-site trauma medical response.
    - Reference: Papazoglou, Aneziris, Bellamy, Ale, and Oh (*Reliability Engineering & System Safety*).
  - **Hit by Object in EOT Crane Operation:**
    - *Top Event:* Load detachment or dropped object from crane.
    - *Preventive Barriers:* Rated rigging inspection, limit switches, certified crane operator.
    - *Mitigative Barriers:* Exclusion zone demarcations, sirens/strobe alarms, hard hats.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Barrier Type | Implementation | Reliability | Maintenance Need |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engineered (Hardware)** | Physical interlocks, relief valves | Highest (predictable) | Regular calibration & testing |
| **Administrative** | Standard operating procedures, work permits | Moderate (process dependent) | Periodic audits & document reviews |
| **Human / Behavioral** | PPE usage, visual inspections | Variable (human error prone) | Continuous training & supervision |

## 4. Must Remember
- Hardware barriers generally demonstrate higher reliability than administrative or behavioral barriers.
- A robust safety system implements multiple independent layers of defense (**Defense in Depth**).
