# Week 1 – Lecture 3: Key concepts and terminologies-Safety domain ontology

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Hazard Mishap Actuation:** The transition from a hazard to an accident occurring when hazard elements, initiating mechanisms (IMs), and target/threat align in time.
- **Entropy Risk Model:** A model explaining that total risk consists of two components:
  - $\text{Total Risk} = \text{Residual Risk} + \text{Entropy Risk}$
  - *Residual Risk:* Risk inherently built into the system by design.
  - *Entropy Risk:* Risk that increases over time as system components (process, technology, human, environment) deteriorate until a critical threshold is reached.
- **Safety Indicators:**
  - *Lagging Indicators:* Post-event metrics reflecting failures that have already occurred (e.g., Lost Time Injuries [LTI], fatalities, failed control systems).
  - *Leading Indicators:* Proactive health metrics of risk control systems used to predict and prevent lagging outcomes.

## 2. Important Points
- **Causal Factor Hierarchy:**
  - *Level 1:* Hazard triangle elements (Hazard Element, Initiating Mechanism, Target & Threat).
  - *Level 2:* Broad system elements (Process, technology/hardware, human, environment).
  - *Level 3:* Specific failure modes and root factors (pump/motor failure modes, software error, human error, timing error, exposure/protection/proximity issues).
- **Accident Causation Path:** Formed as: $\text{Hazardous Element} \rightarrow \text{Sequence of Initiating Mechanisms} \rightarrow \text{Accident} \rightarrow \text{Threats (Fatality, Injury, Damage)} \rightarrow \text{Targets (People, Property, Environment)}$.
- **Risk Control Types:**
  - *Preventive Controls:* Implemented before the accident to break the causal path.
  - *Mitigative Controls:* Implemented after the accident occurs to minimize severity.
- **Risk Control Areas:** Covers maintenance, inspection, operating procedures, staff competency, alarms/instrumentation, management of change (MOC), permits to work (PTW), and emergency arrangements.

## 3. Differences / Examples
### Working-at-Height Study Example (90 Observations)
- **Hazard Elements Identified:** Carrying loads at height, areas requiring work permits/preventive measures, specific job types.
- **Initiating Mechanisms (IMs):** Inappropriate anchoring, damaged/underrated structures, unsafe work ethics, natural events, absence of structural protection, unsafe working conditions, improper object handling/fixing.
- **Outcome:** Lack of risk control system data prevented breaking the alignment between IMs and hazards.

## 4. Must Remember
- Total system risk over time: $\text{Residual Risk} + \text{Entropy Risk}$.
- Reference text cited: *Hazard Analysis Techniques for System Safety* by Clifton A. Ericson II.
- Aligning causal holes reflects Reason's Swiss Cheese Model.
- Targets always belong to: People, Property, or Environment.
