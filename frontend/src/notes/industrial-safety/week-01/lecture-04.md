# Week 1 – Lecture 4: Key concepts and terminologies-Risk Assessment & Control

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Individual Risk (IR):** The frequency with which an individual within a specific group/population sustains a given level of harm from specific hazards ($Risk = Probability \times Consequence$).
- **Societal Risk:** The frequency and number of people suffering a given level of harm from specific hazards; measured using an **F-N curve** (plots cumulative frequency $F$ of exceeding $N$ fatalities vs. number of fatalities $N$).
- **Risk Assessment Process:** Systematic process involving hazard identification, risk quantification (likelihood via cause models $\times$ consequence via effect models), and risk evaluation.
- **Risk Perception:** Psychological factor (gamma parameter) where perceived risk differs from statistical risk (e.g., public perceives nuclear risks higher than common road risks).
- **ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable):** Framework defining risk acceptability across three regions: *Intolerable*, *Tolerable* (cost of reduction exceeds improvement gain), and *Broadly Acceptable*.
- **Prevention through Design (PtD):** Addressing and mitigating hazards for the entire system lifecycle (SLC) during the initial design phase to lower intervention costs and ease implementation.

## 2. Important Points
- **Risk Assessment Approaches:**
  - *Qualitative:* Used initially across entire plants to generate qualitative risk rankings.
  - *Quantitative:* Used for safety-critical subsystems; provides quantified benefits but is costly and time-consuming.
- **ALARP Criteria Boundaries (e.g., Dutch criteria):**
  - Fatality risk $> 10^{-4}$: Intolerable region.
  - Between $10^{-4}$ and $10^{-6}$: Tolerable region (ALARP).
  - Fatality risk $< 10^{-6}$: Broadly acceptable region.
- **System Lifecycle (SLC) Stages:** Business concept $\rightarrow$ Design (preliminary and detailed) $\rightarrow$ Build/Erection $\rightarrow$ Operation and Maintenance $\rightarrow$ Decommissioning/Disposal.
- Designing safety upstream minimizes retrofit requirements and aims for **zero accidents** under Industry 4.0.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Metric | Focus | Representation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Individual Risk (IR)** | Specific individual/job/location exposure | $P \times C$ per person or job group |
| **Societal Risk** | Groups, populations, and community at large | F-N Curve |

## 4. Must Remember
- **Risk Formula:** $R = P \times C$ (Probability/Likelihood $\times$ Consequence/Impact).
- **PtD Principle:** Moving safety prevention to the upstream lifecycle phase (design stage) maximizes ease of implementation and minimizes cost of intervention.
