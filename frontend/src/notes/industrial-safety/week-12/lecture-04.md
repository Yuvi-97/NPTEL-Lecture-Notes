# Week 12 – Lecture 4: Course Summary

## 1. Key Concepts
- **Multidisciplinary Safety Engineering:** A holistic discipline synthesizing engineering design, probabilistic reliability mathematics, human factors, data analytics, management systems, and virtual simulation.
- **Unified Life-Cycle Safety Philosophy:** Integrating hazard identification, risk assessment, engineering control, and continuous monitoring from early concept design through decommissioning.
- **Prevention through Design (PtD):** The supreme safety principle prioritizing the elimination of hazards at the drafting board over administrative rules and personal protective gear.

## 2. Important Points
- **The Five Core Pillars of the Curriculum:**
  1. **Hazard Identification:** Qualitative screening techniques including Preliminary Hazard List (PHL), Preliminary Hazard Analysis (PHA), HAZOP (guidewords), and Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA).
  2. **Risk & Reliability Quantification:**
     - Fault Tree Analysis (FTA): Gate-by-gate, MOCUS minimal cut-sets, and Birnbaum/Fussell-Vesely importance measures.
     - Event Tree Analysis (ETA) and Bow-Tie modeling linking causes to consequence sequences.
     - Basic event lifetime models: Exponential ($h=\lambda$), Weibull ($\beta$ shape parameter for infant mortality, random, and wear-out), Markov processes, and Laplace transforms.
  3. **Risk Control & Decision Making:**
     - William Haddon’s Energy Release Model and the 5-tier Hazard Control Hierarchy.
     - Safety Function Deployment (SFD) and Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) for multi-criteria ranking of safety interventions.
  4. **Human Factors & Data Analytics:**
     - James Reason's GEMS (slips, lapses, mistakes, violations) and Rasmussen's SRK framework.
     - HRA methods (THERP, HEART, and Fuzzy Set quantification).
     - Accident investigation, SPC Control Charts ($c, u, p$ charts), Regression, and Classification Trees (CART).
  5. **Management & Future Technologies:**
     - OHSAS 18001 / ISO 45001 PDCA loop, Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) energy isolation, Leading/Lagging KPIs, and Virtual Reality (VR) safety training.

## 3. Differences / Examples
| Course Dimension | Qualitative Approaches | Quantitative Approaches |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hazard Identification** | HAZOP, PHL, FMEA rankings | FTA cut-set probabilities, basic event failure rates |
| **Decision Formulation** | Safety committees, expert audits | AHP pairwise matrices, risk-cost optimization |
| **Performance Evaluation**| Near-miss incident walkthroughs | Statistical Process Control charts, TRIR/LTIFR indicators |
